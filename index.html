The framework

You already run two kinds of work.

System 2 · Deliberate

Your first time driving

Every mirror, every signal, every gap takes your full attention.

System 1 · Automatic

Driving home tonight

The same decisions. You won’t remember making them.

A business works the same way. Most steps are routine. A few deserve your best people’s full attention.

The framework · one process

Today, a person touches every step.

A customer request, from first email to invoice. Twenty steps, and someone’s hands are on every one.

First, delete the steps that shouldn’t exist.

Forwarding, re-keying, waiting and updating the tracker. Five steps go before any AI is involved.

Then give each step the right kind of help.

Rules and math go to code. Reading and drafting go to AI. Decisions stay with people.

Two steps need a person. They’re the ones that matter.

Same work, same standard. Your people spend their time on price, terms and exceptions.

GoneSteps nobody needs once the work is connected.

CodeRules, lookups and exact math. Same answer every time.

AIReads messy information, compares, drafts for review.

PersonDecisions, exceptions, relationships. Accountable.

Your company

How would you find one in yours?

Start by seeing how the work actually happens, not how it’s written down.

Discover the real work

Look in three places.

People

Sit with the people who run it.

  • Why does this rule exist?
  • Who actually decides?
  • What are the exceptions?
  • What keeps it moving when it breaks?
AI-assisted Systems

Read the real records.

AI can read years of records in minutes.

  • How much comes through
  • What gets corrected
  • Where it changes hands
  • How long it waits
AI-assisted Around the systems

Find the workarounds.

AI can sort the files, inboxes and threads.

  • The spreadsheets
  • The email threads
  • The group chats
  • The SOPs nobody updates

People explain why. AI can mine the records and workarounds for what actually happens, and people confirm what it finds.

Find your bottleneck

Four signs work is stuck.

01

It waits on one expert.

Nothing moves until one person has looked at it.

02

It gets typed twice.

The same numbers move from email to spreadsheet to system.

03

It falls through the cracks.

Follow-ups, handoffs and requests nobody owns.

04

It needs a search party.

Deciding means digging through systems, files and inboxes.

A sensible first move

Prove it before you trust it.

1

Test it on finished work.

Run it on last month’s jobs, where you already know the right answer.

2

Compare it with your expert.

Where they disagree, find out who’s right and why.

3

Start read-only.

It can look and prepare. It can’t change anything yet.

4

A person approves what counts.

Anything that changes a record, commits to a customer or spends money waits for a yes.
